Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Baybex AI ("we", "us") provides software that lets businesses ("customers") send automatic AI-written replies to their own customers on Facebook Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp. This policy explains what data we handle and why. Website: baybexai.digital. Contact: support@baybexai.digital.

1. Who is who

When a business signs up, it is our customer. When that business's own customers message it, we process those messages on the business's behalf. The business decides what is said and is responsible for its own customer relationships.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data from businesses: business name, email, phone and WhatsApp number, password (stored hashed), plan and payment references (such as a bKash transaction ID).
  • Connected channel data: the Facebook Page ID, Instagram account and WhatsApp number a business connects, and the access tokens Meta issues for them.
  • Conversation data: messages sent to a connected Page or number, the sender's platform ID and display name, message text and images/voice notes they send, and the replies we send.
  • Order data: orders a conversation produces, such as items, name, phone and delivery address the shopper provides.
  • Usage data: reply counts, AI usage and cost, and technical logs.

3. How we use it

  • To receive messages and send replies on the business's behalf.
  • To generate replies with AI providers (see section 4).
  • To show the business its conversations, orders and analytics.
  • To bill, secure and improve the service, and to prevent abuse.

We do not sell personal data and do not use conversation content for advertising.

4. Sharing

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp) to send and receive messages.
  • AI providers (such as OpenAI, Google or Anthropic, depending on configuration). Message text, and images or audio when sent, is passed to them only to produce a reply.
  • Hosting providers that run our servers and database.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

5. Automated replies

Replies are generated by AI. Businesses can enable a notice telling people they are chatting with an automated assistant, and can take over any conversation manually.

6. Retention

We keep conversation and order data while the business's account is active so the business can use it. A business may delete data or close its account at any time, and we remove associated data within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal or billing reasons.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed, connections use HTTPS, webhooks from Meta are signature-verified, and access is limited per customer account. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data. If you are a shopper who messaged a business, contact that business first, or write to us and we will help. See Data Deletion.

9. Children

The service is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
