Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 7 November 2026

You can have your data removed from Baybex AI in either of these ways.

If you are a business using Baybex AI

  • Log in at /admin and disconnect your Facebook Page or WhatsApp number in Settings. This stops us receiving new messages and removes the stored access token.
  • To delete your account and all conversations, orders and products, email support@baybexai.digital from your registered email with the subject "Delete my account". We complete it within 30 days.

If you are a person who messaged a business that uses Baybex AI

Email support@baybexai.digital with subject "Delete my messages" and tell us the Page or number you messaged and your name as shown on Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp. We will delete the matching conversation records within 30 days and confirm by email.

Removing the app from Facebook

You can also remove Baybex AI under Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites. A business that does this disconnects its Page; to delete stored data too, email us as above.
